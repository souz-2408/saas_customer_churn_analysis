Executive Summary
An end-to-end business intelligence and churn analysis project examining **250,000 customer records** to identify key retention bottlenecks and operational churn triggers. Overall baseline churn sits at **30.60%**, with specific user segments showing churn rates near **50%**.

---

Key Business Insights

1. **Contract Vulnerability:** Month-to-Month subscribers suffer from an alarming ~50% churn rate compared to annual contract holders[cite: 1].
2. **Support Ticket Tipping Point:** Customer churn spikes drastically at **4 support calls**[cite: 1]. Calls 1 through 3 indicate normal onboarding friction, but ticket #4 represents the critical failure point.

Strategic Recommendations

* **Automated Intervention:** Implement a CRM rule to trigger proactive account outreach upon a user logging their **3rd support ticket**[cite: 1].
* **Contract Incentives:** Offer targeted 10% discounts to convert high-risk Month-to-Month subscribers into Annual plans[cite: 1].
